Good to Know

Quick answers

What are the hours?
Bookings start from 6 AM every day. Sunday to Thursday the room is available around the clock. Friday and Saturday events must end by 2 AM. Minimum booking is 3 hours.
Can we bring food and drinks?
Yes. In house catering is available and the venue can provide alcohol, or you can bring your own. Ask the host about outside catering when you send your request.
Can we bring our own DJ or music?
Absolutely. There's a DJ booth, a PA system and speakers. Bring your own DJ or plug in your playlist. Extra party lights and a fog machine are available for an additional charge.
Is there parking?
Yes, 100 parking spots on the property, with space for trucks and motorhomes too.
What's the cancellation policy?
Cancel within 24 hours of your booking being confirmed for a full refund, as long as the event is more than 48 hours away. Your security deposit is returned within 48 hours after the event.
Where is it located?
Orlando, Florida. The exact address is shared once your booking is confirmed.
